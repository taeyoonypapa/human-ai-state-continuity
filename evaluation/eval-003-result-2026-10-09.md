## Interpretation and limitations

- These tests assess responses to synthetic transcripts, **not** memory reconstruction across real session boundaries.
- The scenarios contain explicit evidence of OPEN or COMPLETE. Both arms identifying it cannot establish a benefit of the extra closure-check instruction.
- Four first replies do not estimate a general error rate or justify statistical inference.
- The instruction itself varies between arms; this is not a test of a persistent Current Work State system.
- Model version, session independence, tool settings and prompts as actually submitted remain user-reported rather than independently audited.
- N1-B0 mentions a device being "available Friday" on the basis of a Thursday statement that it was unavailable "until tomorrow"; treat this as conditional rather than independently verified readiness.

## Across EVAL-001–003

EVAL-001: 1/2 expected classifications, with a false COMPLETE in the partial-reconstruction arm.  
EVAL-002: 6/6 expected classifications; both B0 and C1 were 3/3.  
EVAL-003: 4/4 expected classifications; both B0 and C1 were 2/2.  
These are **12 selected model replies, not an estimate of population performance**. No reduction in false completion attributable to the closure-evidence instruction has been demonstrated.

**Next research step:** test genuinely interrupted, long-horizon collaborative work with independent authoritative state evidence, rather than only asking models to classify synthetic transcripts. Pre-register neutral and intervention conditions before collecting responses.

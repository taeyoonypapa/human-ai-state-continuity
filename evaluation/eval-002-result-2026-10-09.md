## Interpretation and limits

- Both arms were correct for all three hypothetical cases. No added benefit of the explicit closure-evidence instruction was observed here.
- U1 explicitly says closure/open-work evidence is missing. That wording may by itself induce UNRESOLVED; EVAL-001 Condition A was less explicit. The studies do not isolate the effect of the instruction.
- The matched-information comparison studies a prompt-level rule, **not** an implemented persistent Current Work State or reconciliation framework.
- The six provided model outputs do not establish a generalized failure rate or comparative effectiveness. Exact model ID, prompt transcript, temperature and session isolation were not independently verified.
- Raw responses are transcribed from user-supplied text; formatting whitespace may differ from original transport.

**Next:** Independent replication on less signposted and adversarial cases, including stale or conflicting state records, and a genuine matched-information comparison with unchanged factual inputs.
